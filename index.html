How it works

From idea to team, in four steps

1

Build your profile

Add your skills, interests, and experience level from the platform's controlled skill list — no duplicate spellings, no guessing what "React" vs "react.js" means to a matching algorithm.

2

Post or browse projects

Post a project with the roles and skills you need, or search the marketplace for projects that fit what you already know.

3

Apply with a match score

Every project shows a transparent match percentage with a skill-by-skill breakdown — you'll know exactly why you're a fit before you apply.

4

Form your team and track progress

Once you're accepted, a lightweight workspace helps the team track tasks and see real progress — not just a group chat that goes quiet after week two.
